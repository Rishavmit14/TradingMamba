## 4. All Types of Liquidity

### 4.1 Pending Orders (V2)
- **Buy Stops** -- Pending orders placed ABOVE current price by breakout traders expecting upward continuation. Clustered above equal highs and resistance levels. Smart Money triggers these to trap breakout traders.
- **Buy Limits** -- Listed as liquidity type in V2 taxonomy.
- **Sell Stops** -- Pending orders placed BELOW current price by breakout traders expecting downward continuation. Clustered below equal lows and support levels. Smart Money hunts sell stops to fuel upward reversals.
- **Sell Limits** -- Listed as liquidity type in V2 taxonomy.

### 4.2 Stop Losses (V2)
- **Above highs** -- Stop losses placed above swing highs by short sellers.
- **Below lows** -- Stop losses placed below swing lows by long traders.
- **At break-even** -- Stop losses moved to entry price by traders trying to protect profits. These cluster at inducement levels where retail entered.

### 4.3 Equal Levels (V2)
- **Equal Highs (Double Tops)** -- Price levels where highs align at approximately the same price. Retail marks as resistance. SMC recognizes as liquidity pool. "Equal highs = retail resistance = buy stops + sell limits." After sweep, expect reversal.
- **Equal Lows (Double Bottoms)** -- Price levels where lows align at approximately the same price. Retail marks as support. SMC recognizes as liquidity pool. "Equal lows = retail support = sell stops + buy limits." After sweep, expect reversal.

### 4.4 Trendline Liquidity (V8)
- Retail traders place stops and orders along trendlines (support and resistance). Smart Money knows these trendline areas accumulate large concentrations of stop losses. Price often violates trendlines with stop hunts before making the real move.

### 4.5 Engineered Liquidity / ENG LIQ (V8, V9)
- A specific area where retail Support and Resistance based traders are being trapped by Smart Money to create concentrated liquidity pools.
- Detection patterns: horizontal consolidation ranges, trendline S/R that gets violated, repeated swing highs/lows, equal highs/lows, areas where retail would place obvious stop losses.
- "Smart Money creates these zones intentionally. These zones are hunted for liquidity before real moves."

### 4.6 Break-Even Orders as Liquidity (V2)
- Break-even orders are stop losses moved to entry price. They cluster at inducement levels. Smart Money sweeps break-evens before the true move. "Break-evens = liquidity pool."

### 4.7 Inducement as Liquidity Pool (V2, V3, V4)
- Inducement itself is a liquidity pool -- the minor pullback where retail traders enter early, set stops, and get swept. "Inducement is a trap BEFORE an area of Order Block, Supply, or Demand."
- Larger inducements = larger liquidity pools = higher Smart Money priority (V8).

## 5. Wick Sweep vs Body Sweep -- The Critical Distinction

### 5.1 Definitions (V3, V6)

**Wick-Based Sweep:**
- Price WICKS through the high/low level.
- Body does NOT close beyond the level.
- Body can be anywhere (inside the range).
- Fully valid for pullback confirmation.
- Indicates Smart Money collected orders briefly.

**Body-Based Sweep:**
- Candle BODY closes beyond the high/low level.
- More decisive move.
- Fully valid for pullback confirmation.
- Indicates stronger institutional conviction.

### 5.2 Where Wick vs Body Matters Differently (V6 -- The Definitive Table)

| Rule | Liquidity Sweep | Break of Structure | Swing High/Low |
|------|-----------------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Rule 1: Inducement** | NOT compulsory | MUST take out IDM | MUST take out IDM |
| **Rule 2: Body close below IDM** | Only wick counts; body close INVALIDATES | Wick OR body both valid | ONLY body close valid; wick INVALID |
| **Rule 3: Body close above high** | Only wick counts; body close INVALIDATES | Body MUST close above high | Body MUST close above high |

**Critical Takeaways:**
- For Liquidity Sweep: ONLY wick counts. If body closes beyond the level, it is NOT a liquidity sweep (it becomes BOS or swing H/L).
- For BOS: Body close is required at the high/low (Rule 3), but Rule 2 (inducement) is flexible (wick or body).
- For Swing H/L: STRICTEST -- both Rule 2 and Rule 3 require body close.

### 5.3 Wick vs Body for Pullback Validation (V3)

> "To check validity, there is only one factor for confirmation. This most important factor is liquidity sweep."
> "Both wick-based AND body-based sweeps are valid" for pullback confirmation.
> "It is not necessary that next candle must close or break the high or low of the sweeping candle."

In a **bullish market** pullback: The LOW of the HIGHEST candle must be broken (by wick or body).
In a **bearish market** pullback: The HIGH of the LOWEST candle must be broken (by wick or body).

### 5.4 Candlestick Anatomy for Sweep Identification (V3, V16)

- **Long wick + small body closing back inside range** = Liquidity Sweep
- **Large body closing beyond range** = Genuine breakout / valid BOS
- **Body = price acceptance and institutional activity** (V16)
- **Wick = liquidity hunt or rejection** (V16)

## 7. How Liquidity Connects to Other SMC Concepts

### 7.1 Liquidity <-> Inducement (IDM)
- **Inducement IS a liquidity pool.** It is the minor pullback where retail traders enter early, set stops, and create concentrated liquidity. (V2)
- **70% of forex market works around inducement** -- which is fundamentally about liquidity collection. (V2)
- **Inducement must be swept (liquidity taken)** before valid HH/HL, BOS, or CHoCH. (V1, V2, V5)
- **Valid inducement requires valid pullback, which requires liquidity sweep.** Circular dependency: without liquidity sweep, pullback is invalid, therefore inducement is invalid. (V3)
- **Inducement is positioned BEFORE Order Blocks** -- Smart Money traps retail at inducement to protect OBs. (V2)

### 7.2 Liquidity <-> Valid Pullback
- **Liquidity sweep is THE ONLY confirmation factor for valid pullback.** (V3)
- Without liquidity sweep, the pullback is invalid and therefore the inducement is fake. (V3)
- Both wick and body sweeps validate pullbacks. (V3)
- Reference candle must be correctly identified (absolute highest/lowest). (V3)

### 7.3 Liquidity <-> Break of Structure (BOS)
- **Liquidity sweep is the opposite of BOS at Rule 3:** wick-only = liquidity sweep, body close = BOS. (V5, V6)
- BOS Rule 1 requires inducement taken (which is itself a liquidity sweep). (V5)
- A liquidity sweep can convert to BOS if next candle body closes beyond the wick high. (V5)
- Without understanding liquidity sweep, traders mark fake BOS everywhere. (V5)

### 7.4 Liquidity <-> Change of Character (CHoCH)
- **Liquidity sweep vs CHoCH:** wick beyond swing + body inside = sweep; body close beyond = CHoCH. (V7)
- Retail traders confuse liquidity sweeps with CHoCH, entering fake reversal trades. (V7)
- Weak swings created by sweeping candles cannot produce valid CHoCH. (V9)
- Lower TF swing may be higher TF inducement (liquidity) -- breaking it = liquidity hunt, not CHoCH. (V9, Rule 4)

### 7.5 Liquidity <-> Fair Value Gap (FVG)
- FVGs form during strong impulse moves -- often the same moves that sweep liquidity. (V13)
- Price imbalances (FVG) may require deeper sweeps of multiple inducements. (V4)
- FVG entry timing aligns with post-liquidity-sweep reversal points. (V13)
- Combined FVG + liquidity sweep = higher probability entry. (V15)

### 7.6 Liquidity <-> Order Block (OB)
- **Order Blocks are PROTECTED by inducement (liquidity traps).** Retail is prevented from buying/selling at OB by being trapped at inducement first. (V2)
- Inducement sweep must occur before OB is tapped. (V2)
- Sequence: Inducement (liquidity) -> Sweep -> Order Block -> Entry. (V2, V14)
- OB mitigation = price returning to absorb institutional orders (similar to filling liquidity). (V14)
- Deeper OBs attract sweeps of multiple inducement (liquidity) levels. (V4)

### 7.7 Liquidity <-> Engineered Liquidity (ENG LIQ)
- ENG LIQ is deliberately created liquidity -- zones where retail S/R traders are systematically trapped. (V8)
- Types: consolidation ranges, trendlines, repeated highs/lows, S/R zones. (V8)
- Fake CHoCH creates ENG LIQ zones to trap more retail traders. (V9)
- Smart Money hunts ENG LIQ before making real directional moves. (V8)
- Hierarchy: Large inducements > ENG LIQ > Equal H/L > Recent IDM > Small pullbacks. (V8)

### 7.8 Liquidity <-> Premium/Discount Zones
- Liquidity sweeps in discount zones provide optimal long entries. (V12)
- Liquidity sweeps in premium zones provide optimal short entries. (V12)
- Kill zones (session timing) + premium/discount + liquidity sweep = highest confluence. (V15, V16)

### 7.9 Liquidity <-> Price Cycle Theory
- **Accumulation phase** = Smart Money quietly building positions (engineering liquidity at lows). (V11)
- **Distribution phase** = Smart Money distributing to retail (engineering liquidity at highs). (V11)
- Markup and markdown phases are expansion after liquidity has been collected. (V11)

classify_sweep_type(candle, level, direction):

### 9.5 Liquidity Sweep vs BOS vs Swing H/L Classifier (V6 table)

```
classify_structural_event(candle, next_candle, inducement_taken, inducement_body_close, level):
    # Apply V6 3-rule table

    wick_beyond = (candle.high > level and candle.close < level)  # for highs
    body_beyond = candle.close > level  # for highs

    # LIQUIDITY SWEEP (no inducement needed, wick only)
    if wick_beyond and not body_beyond:
        # Check next candle for potential BOS conversion
        if next_candle and next_candle.close > candle.high:
            return "BOS_CONVERTED"  # Was sweep, next candle confirmed BOS
        return "LIQUIDITY_SWEEP"

    # Check BOS (inducement required, body close required)
    if inducement_taken and body_beyond:
        return "BREAK_OF_STRUCTURE"

    # Check Swing H/L (inducement + body close on IDM + body close above high)
    if inducement_taken and inducement_body_close and body_beyond:
        return "SWING_HIGH_LOW"

    # Not a valid BOS (inducement not taken)
    if not inducement_taken and body_beyond:
        return "FAKE_BOS"

    return "NO_EVENT"
```

## 12. Summary -- The Essential Liquidity Framework

### What Liquidity IS:
Pools of stop losses, pending orders, break-even orders, and trapped retail positions that Smart Money targets to fill institutional orders.

### What Liquidity Sweep IS:
Price briefly moving beyond a level (via wick) to trigger these orders, then closing back inside the range. It is NOT a breakout -- it is a trap. Characterized by speed and efficiency (institutional footprint).

### The Universal Rule:
**Wick beyond level + body closes inside = LIQUIDITY SWEEP (not valid structural break).**
**Body closes beyond level = VALID BREAK (BOS, CHoCH, or Swing H/L depending on other rules).**

### The Hierarchy of Importance:
1. Liquidity sweep is the ONLY factor that validates pullbacks (V3).
2. Inducement must be swept (liquidity taken) for valid structure (V1, V2, V5).
3. Liquidity sweep vs BOS is distinguished by body close rule (V5, V6).
4. Engineered Liquidity zones are deliberately created traps (V8).
5. Larger, fresher liquidity pools are hunted first (V8).
6. Kill zones (London/NY overlap) are when major liquidity sweeps happen (V16).

### The Trading Implication:
Never be PART of the liquidity. Understand WHERE liquidity pools exist, WHEN they will be swept, and trade WITH Smart Money AFTER the sweep completes.
