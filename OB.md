## 1. Core Definition of Order Block

**Order Block (OB)** is the last opposite candle before a Break of Structure (BOS). Specifically:
- **Bullish Order Block** = the last BEARISH (down/red) candle before a bullish BOS.
- **Bearish Order Block** = the last BULLISH (up/green) candle before a bearish BOS.

Order Blocks represent the zone where **institutional orders were placed** -- Smart Money's buying or selling occurred here before price reversed and broke structure. The candle that precedes the Break of Structure contains the **institutional footprint**.

Order Blocks act as strong support (bullish OB) or resistance (bearish OB) when price returns. The key difference from retail support/resistance: OBs are based on institutional order flow, not arbitrary horizontal lines.

## 3. Bullish OB vs Bearish OB

### Bullish Order Block
- **Definition:** Last bearish (red/down) candle before bullish BOS.
- **Candle Type:** Bearish / down candle.
- **Formation Context:** Before upward Break of Structure.
- **Institutional Meaning:** Smart Money placed LARGE BUY orders here -- the down candle was the last gasp of selling before institutional buying overwhelmed and broke structure upward.
- **Zone:** Entire candle body and wicks form the OB zone.
- **Function:** Acts as SUPPORT when price returns.
- **Trading Action:** BUY when price pulls back to this zone in an uptrend.
- **Why It Works:** Unfilled institutional buy orders remain at this level.

### Bearish Order Block
- **Definition:** Last bullish (green/up) candle before bearish BOS.
- **Candle Type:** Bullish / up candle.
- **Formation Context:** Before downward Break of Structure.
- **Institutional Meaning:** Smart Money placed LARGE SELL orders here -- the up candle was the last rally before institutional selling overwhelmed and broke structure downward.
- **Zone:** Entire candle body and wicks form the OB zone.
- **Function:** Acts as RESISTANCE when price returns.
- **Trading Action:** SELL when price retraces to this zone in a downtrend.
- **Why It Works:** Unfilled institutional sell orders remain at this level.

## 4. Valid vs Invalid OB Criteria

### Valid Order Block Criteria (from V14 - valid_vs_invalid_ob)

A Valid Order Block MUST meet ALL of the following:

| # | Criterion | Explanation |
|---|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | **Precedes a confirmed BOS** | Must be directly associated with a Break of Structure -- not just any opposite candle |
| 2 | **Last opposite candle before BOS** | Specifically the LAST bearish candle (bullish OB) or LAST bullish candle (bearish OB) before the break |
| 3 | **BOS has body close beyond swing** | The BOS itself must be valid (V5 Rule 2: body close above high / below low) |
| 4 | **OB not yet mitigated/violated** | The OB zone must be FRESH -- price has not yet returned to fill it |

### Invalid Order Block Indicators

| # | Indicator | Why Invalid |
|---|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | No BOS association | Candle not preceding any structure break has no institutional significance |
| 2 | Not the last candle before BOS | Earlier candles do not contain the final institutional positioning |
| 3 | Already mitigated/broken | Orders already absorbed -- zone has lost its power |
| 4 | Random candle selection | Retail mistake of marking arbitrary candles as OBs |

### BOS Validation Requirements (from V5 -- prerequisite for valid OB)

The BOS that the OB precedes must itself be valid per the 2-rule system:
- **Rule 1:** Price must take out inducement before BOS (wick or body).
- **Rule 2:** Candle body must close above previous high (bullish) or below previous low (bearish).

If the BOS is fake/invalid, the OB associated with it is also invalid.

From Video 2 (inducement_and_order_blocks):

- "Inducement sweep must occur before OB is tapped."

This means a valid OB entry also requires that inducement has been taken first.

## 5. OB Mitigation Lifecycle (Fresh --> Partial --> Full --> Invalid)

### From V14 (ob_mitigation):

**Order Block Mitigation** occurs when price returns to the OB zone and absorbs the institutional orders, reducing or eliminating the OB's power.

### Lifecycle Stages:

```
FRESH (Un-mitigated)
  |
  |  Highest probability
  |  All institutional orders intact
  |  PRIMARY entry zone
  |
  v
PARTIALLY MITIGATED
  |
  |  Price touched OB but did not fully penetrate
  |  Reduced but still active
  |  Can still provide entries (lower probability)
  |
  v
FULLY MITIGATED
  |
  |  Price completely filled/penetrated OB zone
  |  Orders absorbed
  |  Zone is "used up"
  |
  v
INVALID
  |
  |  OB broken / violated
  |  Should NOT be used for future entries
  |  Zone has no remaining institutional power
```

### Mitigation Rules:
- **Full mitigation** = price completely fills/penetrates the OB zone.
- **Partial mitigation** = price touches OB but does not fully penetrate.
- **After mitigation**, OB loses strength and should NOT be used for future entries.
- **Fresh (un-mitigated) OBs** have highest probability.
- **Track OB status:** Fresh --> Partially Mitigated --> Fully Mitigated --> Invalid.

### Trading Implications:
- Only trade FRESH OBs for highest probability.
- Partially mitigated OBs can be used with reduced confidence.
- Fully mitigated OBs should be removed from charts.
- Invalid OBs should never be traded.

## 6. OB + BOS Connection (Last Opposite Candle Before BOS)

### The Fundamental Relationship

The Order Block IS the BOS. More precisely:
- OB is the **genesis candle** of the BOS -- the last candle of the old order before institutional force breaks structure.
- Without BOS, there is no OB (just an ordinary candle).
- Without the OB candle, there is no institutional footprint to anchor the zone.

### How BOS Creates OB (from V5 + V14):

**Bullish Scenario:**
```
... bearish candle [THIS IS THE BULLISH OB] ...
... price breaks above previous swing high (BOS) ...
... body closes above high (V5 Rule 2 confirmed) ...
```
The last bearish candle before this confirmed bullish BOS = Bullish Order Block.

**Bearish Scenario:**
```
... bullish candle [THIS IS THE BEARISH OB] ...
... price breaks below previous swing low (BOS) ...
... body closes below low (V5 Rule 2 confirmed) ...
```
The last bullish candle before this confirmed bearish BOS = Bearish Order Block.

### BOS Validation Chain (V5 --> V14):

For OB to be valid, the BOS must pass BOTH V5 rules:
1. **V5 Rule 1:** Price must take out inducement before BOS.
2. **V5 Rule 2:** Candle body must close above high (bullish) or below low (bearish).

If either rule fails:
- The BOS is fake/invalid.
- The "OB" associated with it is NOT a valid Order Block.
- Do NOT trade it.

## 7. OB Trading Strategy Steps

### Basic Strategy (from V14 - ob_trading_strategy):

1. **Confirm trend direction** -- structure mapping (HH/HL or LL/LH).
2. **Identify BOS** in trend direction (validated with V5 two rules).
3. **Mark last opposite candle** as Order Block (entire candle = zone).
4. **Wait for retracement** to OB zone (patience).
5. **Enter at OB zone** when price touches it.
6. **Stop loss beyond OB** (below OB for longs, above OB for shorts).
7. **Target** next structure/swing level.

### Enhanced Strategy (combining V2 + V5 + V12 + V13 + V14 + V15):

1. **Trend confirmed** via structure mapping (V1-V7).
2. **Inducement identified and taken** (V2-V4) -- OB is now accessible.
3. **BOS confirmed** with both rules (V5).
4. **Last opposite candle marked as OB** (V14).
5. **Check FVG overlap** -- does an FVG coincide with the OB? (V13+V14 confluence).
6. **Verify premium/discount** -- is OB in the correct zone? (V12).
   - Bullish OB should be in discount zone (lower 50%).
   - Bearish OB should be in premium zone (upper 50%).
7. **Wait for price to retrace** to OB+FVG confluence zone.
8. **Enter at the zone** during London/NY kill zone (V16).
9. **Stop beyond OB**, target next structural level.
