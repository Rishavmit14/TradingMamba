## SWING POINT FOUNDATION

*Teaching time: 4.0 min | Words: 800 | Frames: 2*

**Swing point = minimum 3-candle pattern where middle candle sweeps previous candle's liquidity and third candle closes beyond the opposite extreme.**

- High Swing Point: middle candle sweeps previous high → third candle closes below middle candle's low
- Low Swing Point: middle candle sweeps previous low → third candle closes above middle candle's high
- Middle candle's extreme (high for High SP, low for Low SP) MUST remain protected
- Protected = price does not return beyond that extreme
- Candle colors do NOT matter — only the pattern matters
- Two sweep types: wick sweep (sweep and close inside) or body close beyond (stronger)

## SCOB DEFINITION

*Teaching time: 4.0 min | Words: 800 | Frames: 1*

**SCOB (Single Candle Order Block) = entry confirmation technique based on swing points. The middle candle of a valid swing point IS the SCOB candle.**

- Also called SCOOP (Single Candle Order Block)
- Bullish SCOB: middle candle sweeps previous low → next candle closes above middle high
- Bearish SCOB: middle candle sweeps previous high → next candle closes below middle low
- SCOB ONLY valid when forming at significant level (previous high/low sweep, FVG, OB)
- POI zone priority for SCOB: FVG (1st) → IOF Candle (2nd) → Order Block (3rd)
- Entry: at SCOB candle's retest | SL: beyond SCOB candle's extreme | TP: previous structure low/high

## SCOB TYPES AND VARIATIONS

*Teaching time: 5.0 min | Words: 900 | Frames: 1*

**SCOB can consist of 3 to 5 candles depending on conditions. Standard (3), Extended (4-5), Inside Candle variation, and High Probability with FVG.**

- Standard SCOB = 3 candles (middle sweeps, third closes beyond)
- Extended SCOB = 4-5 candles (third candle fails to close → wait for fourth)
- Inside candle variation: skip inside candle → mark the first sweeping candle as SCOB
- High Probability SCOB: FVG exists immediately below/above the SCOB candle
- FVG below bearish SCOB = high probability (better risk/reward)
- FVG above bullish SCOB = high probability (better risk/reward)
- Without FVG: SCOB is still valid but lower probability
- 4 standard bearish SCOB patterns + 4 high probability versions with FVG
