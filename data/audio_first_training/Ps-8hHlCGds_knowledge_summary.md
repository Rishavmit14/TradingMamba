## Overview

This is a weekly webinar focused on **entry techniques**, specifically the **Market Structure Shift (MSS)** and its quality grading system. The instructor introduces a 3-tier MSS classification: Standard (50%), A+ (65%), and A++ (75-85%), based on what forms in the structure shift leg. The video also covers trading psychology, the rule of thumb for liquidity, multi-timeframe MSS entry, and reveals patterns stronger than A++ (CISD and Wyckoff).

## Key Concepts

### 1. MSS Grading System (The Core Teaching)

| Grade | Elements | Win Rate |
|-------|----------|----------|
| **Standard MSS** | Liquidity sweep + MSS + FVG | ~50% |
| **A+ MSS** | Standard + iFVG (inverted sell FVG) | ~65% |
| **A++ MSS** | Standard + BPR (Balanced Price Range) | ~75-85% |
| **A++ with extra FVG** | A++ + additional FVG inside BPR | ~85-90% |

**Checklist for evaluating MSS quality:**
1. Liquidity (was opposite-side liquidity swept?)
2. Expansion (displacement/momentum candle?)
3. FVG (fair value gap in the shift leg?)
4. iFVG (inverse FVG from sell leg inversion?)
5. BPR (balanced price range formed?)

### 2. Rule of Thumb — Liquidity First

The golden rule of SMC/ICT:
- **Market wants to BUY** → first sweeps **sell-side liquidity** (SSL below lows)
- **Market wants to SELL** → first sweeps **buy-side liquidity** (BSL above highs)

Without liquidity as the base, ALL 9 entry techniques fail:
1. Breaker Block
2. Mitigation Block
3. Unicorn Model
4. Silver Bullet
5. CRT
6. POI Zone > CoC
7. **Market Structure Shift** (base of most techniques)
8. Sweep Based CoC
9. Flip Module

### 3. Multi-Timeframe MSS Entry Process

**Never trade MSS on a single timeframe.**

| Step | Timeframe | Action |
|------|-----------|--------|
| 1 | HTF (H4/H1) | Identify trend direction + key zones |
| 2 | HTF | Wait for price to enter zone with SSL/BSL swept |
| 3 | LTF (M15/M5) | Switch to lower timeframe |
| 4 | LTF | Wait for LTF liquidity sweep |
| 5 | LTF | Look for MSS (structure shift) |
| 6 | LTF | Enter on FVG retest within MSS leg |
| 7 | — | SL = 2-3 pips below MSS pivot low |
| 8 | HTF | TP = most recent BSL/SSL on HTF |

### 4. FVG = The Identity of MSS

- FVG is the **biggest identity** of a valid MSS
- If the shift leg has NO FVG → MSS is weak and likely fails
- FVG represents displacement/expansion = smart money footprint
- No other POI can replace FVG for MSS entry
- FVG provides both confirmation AND entry level

### 5. iFVG (Inverse Fair Value Gap)

What separates A+ from Standard MSS:
- Before MSS, a sell leg created a bearish FVG
- During MSS expansion, market closes THROUGH the sell FVG → **inverts it**
- iFVG becomes a demand/support zone
- iFVG + buy FVG = "cumulative zone" with higher probability
- Retail mistake: selling at sell FVG while neglecting HTF buy trend

### 6. BPR (Balanced Price Range)

What makes A++ MSS the strongest:
- BPR = iFVG and buy FVG overlap at the **same price level**
- Both buyer and seller imbalances converge at one point
- Ultimate institutional confluence
- A++ with BPR = 75-80%, with extra FVG = 85-90%

### 7. Beyond A++ — CISD & Wyckoff

Even stronger than A++ MSS:
- **CISD** (Change in State of Delivery) — fundamental shift in price delivery
- **Wyckoff Rules** — specifically the 3-Bar Reversal Pattern
- Reference: Webinar #22 (video newJA7qOzpE) for Wyckoff SBC module
- CISD + Wyckoff alignment = absolute strongest MSS confirmation

### 8. Psychology — Stop-Losing Mindset

- Reframe "stop loss" → "stop losing" for positive trading psychology
- Stop loss = open order that invites the market (retail creates own liquidity)
- Trading is a minimum 6-month painful journey — no shortcuts
- Entry techniques begin with understanding where retail stop losses cluster
