## CONSOLIDATION (Phase 1 — Beginning of Everything)

*Teaching time: 4.0 min | Words: 600 | Frames: 1*

**Consolidation = price ranging between two defined points. It's the BEGINNING of the cycle.**

- Indicates LACK of trend (trading range)
- Creates **Liquidity on BOTH sides** — breakout traders place pending orders above/below
- More consolidation → more liquidity → **stronger subsequent move**
- After consolidation → always expect a **BIG MOVE**
- Retail sees resistance (top) and support (bottom) → their orders become SM's fuel

## EXPANSION (Phase 2 — Smart Money Footprints)

*Teaching time: 4.0 min | Words: 500 | Frames: 1*

**Expansion = trend continuation (BOS). Quick price move with speed/displacement after consolidation.**

- Indicates **footprints of Smart Money** entering the market
- Entry zones found **NEAR THE START of expansion**:
  - Bullish/Bearish Order Blocks
  - Liquidity Voids / Price Imbalance
  - Supply/Demand Zones
  - Fair Value Gaps
- Where expansion starts = where SM entered = **best entry point**

## RETRACEMENT (Phase 3 — Correction/Pullback)

*Teaching time: 4.0 min | Words: 500 | Frames: 1*

**Retracement = pullbacks to correct price imbalances (FVGs/Liquidity Voids) after expansion.**

- Mostly occurs AFTER expansion
- Purpose: balance the imbalance created by expansion
- Expansion creates zones with only buy OR sell candles = price imbalance
- Market retraces to fill/correct, then continues original direction
- Retracement ≠ Reversal — it's correction, NOT trend change

*Teaching time: 8.0 min | Words: 1,200 | Frames: 2*

**The 4 phases form a cycle with specific allowed and forbidden transitions.**

### The Cycle:
```
Consolidation (Range) ↔ Expansion (BOS) ↔ Retracement (Pullback) ↔ Reversal (CHoCH)
```

### Allowed Transitions (bidirectional):
| From | To |
|------|-----|
| Consolidation | Expansion |

### FORBIDDEN Transitions:
| From | To | Why |
