## Concepts Covered (5)

### 1. Complete SMC Trading Framework
**BOS vs Swing Points:**
- **BOS** = IDM sweep + close above/below = trend continuation ONLY
- **Swing Points** = IDM close below + above = critical level + liquidity protection + potential trend change
- Higher Low break = CHoCH; all liquidity below Higher Low is PROTECTED

**Zone Identification:**
1. After BOS → find new IDM (valid pullback on left side)
2. Below IDM → find OB (candle that swept previous candle's liquidity + has FVG)
3. Wait for zone tap + confirmation before entry
4. BOS leg zones ARE tradeable (left-side liquidity was swept)
5. Internal candles NEVER provide inducement

### 2. Entry Techniques — Same TF & Multi-TF

| Method | When | How | R:R |
|--------|------|-----|-----|
| Same TF | Zone tapped on M15 | Wait valid pullback break | ~1:1 to 1:2 |
| Multi-TF | Zone tapped on M15 | Switch to M5, find MSS | **1:6.43** |
| SCOB | Zone tapped + rejection | Trade SCOB break | Variable |

**SCOB Rules (CRITICAL):**
- SCOB is ONLY valid when market taps a POI zone OR sweeps high/low/IDM
- SCOB from random location = INVALID

**Stop Loss:**
- NEVER at immediate high/low (market hunts these)
- Always 2-4 pips beyond the sell/buy zone
- For direct trades: SL at Lower High / Higher Low point

### 3. Without Inducement — AMD / Power of Three
When market doesn't take IDM but creates an internal range:
1. Market approaches zone → forms RANGE instead of continuing
2. Internal liquidity builds on both sides of range
3. Internal low/high swept (Manipulation phase)
4. MSS after internal sweep → ENTRY
5. **Important**: BOS from AMD is NOT valid (no left-side IDM sweep)

### 4. Climax Detection & CHoCH Direction Switch

**Climax:**
- Largest single move in current trend = CLIMACTIC
- After climax → become vigilant → require proper confirmation
- Don't take direct entries after climax

**CHoCH Direction Switch Protocol:**
1. Identify climactic move (largest in trend)
2. Market breaks Lower High (sell trend) or Higher Low (buy trend) → CHoCH
3. Climax + CHoCH = strong direction switch signal (two confluences)
4. Switch to new direction → find new IDM → new zone → wait for MSS → enter

**Counter-Trend (Revisited):**
- ONLY after: BOS + candle close below/above major IDM + FVG in leg
- TP = first opposing zone ONLY (don't expect full reversal)
- QML + OB = highest probability counter-trend entry

## Master Trading Checklist (From This Lecture)
```
1. IDENTIFY TREND: Is market making HH/HL (buy) or LH/LL (sell)?
2. MARK SWING POINTS: Where are Higher Lows / Lower Highs? (These = liquidity protection)
3. FIND IDM: After each BOS, locate the new inducement (valid pullback on left side)
4. FIND ZONE: Below IDM → OB that swept previous candle + has FVG = sell/buy zone
5. WAIT FOR TAP: Don't enter until market actually reaches your zone
6. CONFIRM ENTRY:
   - Same TF: Valid pullback break after zone tap
   - Multi-TF: M5 MSS / structure shift after zone tap
   - SCOB: Only at POI zone or after liquidity sweep
7. SET SL: 2-4 pips beyond sell/buy zone (never at immediate extreme)
8. SET TP: Previous high/low from which market took inducement
9. MONITOR FOR CLIMAX: If largest move in trend → be vigilant
10. DETECT CHoCH: If Lower High / Higher Low breaks → potential direction switch
11. COUNTER-TREND: Only when BOS + IDM close + FVG all present → TP first opposing zone
```
