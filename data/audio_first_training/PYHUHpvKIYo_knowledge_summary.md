## INDUCEMENT SHIFT RULE 1 (No Sweep)

*Teaching time: 5.0 min | Words: 600 | Frames: 1*

**When market does NOT sweep inducement and creates a new high, inducement SHIFTS.**

### Process:
1. Market creates High → Low → New High
2. Inducement is at the first valid pullback on the left side of the high
3. Market moves up WITHOUT sweeping this inducement
4. Market creates a NEW higher high
5. Old inducement shifts → new inducement = first valid pullback on left side of NEW high

### Key Points:
- Old inducement still exists but importance diminishes significantly
- New inducement must be a VALID pullback (highest candle's low must be broken — V03 rule)
- When the shifted inducement is eventually swept, market reverses from that level

**Visual**: Candlestick chart showing inducement label at pullback level that was NOT swept, with new high formed above.

## INDUCEMENT SHIFT RULE 2 (Sweep Without Close)

*Teaching time: 6.0 min | Words: 800 | Frames: 1*

**When market SWEEPS inducement but candle does NOT close below it, inducement SHIFTS.**

### Process:
1. Market sweeps the inducement level (wick goes through it)
2. But candle body does NOT close below the inducement
3. No candle close = no valid Higher Low formed
4. Market creates a new high
5. The entire swing acts as inducement → inducement shifts

### Multiple Inducement Scenario:
- When inducement shifts, TWO inducement levels can exist simultaneously
- Market may take the FIRST inducement and reverse (Scenario 1)
- Market may take BOTH inducements before reversing (Scenario 2)
- Which scenario occurs depends on entry setup techniques (later lectures)

### Why Market Takes Both:
- Below the inducements: **Imbalance / Liquidity Void** zone exists
- Price always seeks to balance itself → fills imbalance before reversing
- **Order Block** sits below the imbalance → Smart Money's actual entry

**Visual**: Extended forex chart showing shifted inducement, Imbalance/Liquidity Void zone in pink, and Order Block below.

## INDUCEMENT SHIFT RULE 3 — FAKE CHoCH (Most Critical)

*Teaching time: 6.0 min | Words: 700 | Frames: 2*

**This is the MOST IMPORTANT rule. When market sweeps + closes below inducement BUT new high is an IMPULSE SWING, the entire swing acts as inducement.**

### Definition:
An **Impulse Swing** = move from low to high WITHOUT any pullback/correction.

### The Rule:
1. Market sweeps inducement AND candle closes below it ✓
2. Market creates a new high ✓
3. BUT the move from low to high is an IMPULSE (no pullback) ✓
4. → The LOW of this impulse swing is NOT a valid Higher Low
5. → The entire impulse swing IS the inducement
6. → Inducement shifts to this impulse swing's low

### The Fake CHoCH Trap:

| Trader Type | What They See | Action | Result |
|-------------|--------------|--------|--------|
| Retail | CHoCH occurred (low broken) | SELL | Loss (trapped) |
| Smart Money | Impulse swing = inducement, not real CHoCH | BUY | Profit |

- **Retail traders started selling** thinking CHoCH occurred
- **Presenter bought** at order block → market reversed up → new high
- Trade confirmed: fake CHoCH trap, real move was bullish
