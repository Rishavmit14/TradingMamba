## CHoCH DEFINITION (Foundation)

*Teaching time: 3.0 min | Words: 400 | Frames: 1*

**Change of Character (CHoCH) = Indication of Trend Reversal.**

### Bullish → Bearish:
- Market breaks its current **Strong Swing Higher Low (HL)** → CHoCH
- Trend changes from bullish to bearish

### Bearish → Bullish:
- Market breaks its current **Strong Swing Lower High (LH)** → CHoCH
- Trend changes from bearish to bullish

### Properties:
- **Timeframe-dependent**: M15, H1, H4, D1 each have their own CHoCH
- **Smaller timeframe CHoCH occurs FIRST**, then larger timeframe follows
- **Strong vs Weak swing points** determine which HL/LH is valid for CHoCH (next lecture)

## RETAIL VS SMART MONEY CHoCH (Core Teaching)

*Teaching time: 8.0 min | Words: 1,200 | Frames: 2*

**Retail marks CHoCH at every minor low break. Smart Money only marks at MAJOR swing HL break.**

### Retail Trader Approach:
- Marks every high and low as swing points (no rules)
- Every time any low breaks → marks CHoCH → sells
- CHoCH fails repeatedly → market continues bullish
- Marks CHoCH 3-4 times, each time failing
- What they thought was CHoCH was actually **inducement being taken**

### Smart Money Classical Approach:
- First maps valid swing HH/HL using inducement rules (V01-V06)
- Identifies IDM at first valid pullback on left side of each high
- Understands inducement being taken ≠ CHoCH
- **CHoCH ONLY when MAJOR swing Higher Low is broken**
- After CHoCH → market creates LL + LH → confirms reversal

### Impact:
- Failed setups: **50 per 100 → 5-10 per 100** (90% success rate)
