## BULLISH CANDLESTICK FORMATION THEORY

*Teaching time: 6.0 min | Words: 1,200 | Frames: 2*

**How a bullish daily candle forms through the 4 sessions.**

### Formation Sequence:
1. **Asian Session** — Market opens, ranges (consolidation) → builds liquidity on BOTH sides
2. **London Session** — Dips DOWN to sweep Asian low-side liquidity (**Judas Swing / manipulation**) → creates daily **LOW** → reverses UP aggressively
3. **New York Session** — Continues London's bullish direction → creates daily **HIGH**
4. **Sydney Session** — Ranges, price pulls back → creates upper **WICK** → candle **CLOSES**

### Candle Anatomy Mapping:
- **Lower wick** = London's dip and recovery (liquidity sweep area)
- **Body** = London + New York one-directional trending move
- **Upper wick** = New York high to Sydney pullback

### Key Statistics:
- London posts daily LOW ~70% of the time in bullish markets
- New York posts daily HIGH ~70% of the time in bullish markets

**Visual**: Complete formation diagram showing Asian zigzag → London dip → trending body → Sydney close → resulting bullish candle.

## BEARISH CANDLESTICK FORMATION (Inverted)

*Teaching time: 2.0 min | Words: 400 | Frames: 0*

**100% inverted version of bullish formation.**

1. **Asian Session** — Ranges, builds liquidity on both sides
2. **London Session** — Sweeps Asian HIGH-side liquidity (Judas Swing UP) → creates daily **HIGH** → reverses DOWN
3. **New York Session** — Continues bearish direction → creates daily **LOW**
4. **Sydney Session** — Ranges → candle closes

**Bearish**: London = daily HIGH, New York = daily LOW.

## CANDLESTICK OHLC & ANATOMY

*Teaching time: 3.0 min | Words: 500 | Frames: 0*

**OHLC positions and the critical Body vs Wick distinction.**

- **Bullish**: Open at bottom, Close at top
- **Bearish**: Open at top, Close at bottom
- **High**: always at top | **Low**: always at bottom

### Critical Anatomy Insight:
| Part | Represents | Who |
|------|-----------|-----|
| **Body** | Actual trading volume | Smart Money / Banks / Institutions |
| **Wicks** | Attempted moves that got reversed | Retail Traders |

Body = institutional footprint. Wicks = retail footprint. This distinction is essential for reading institutional intent.
