## LIQUIDITY SWEEP vs LIQUIDITY GRAB

*Teaching time: 5.0 min | Words: 800 | Frames: 1*

**Same objective (gain liquidity), different methods.**

| Feature | Liquidity Sweep | Liquidity Grab |
|---------|----------------|----------------|
| Mechanism | Wick sweeps prev high/low | Body closes beyond consolidation range |
| Body Close | NO body close beyond level | YES body close beyond range |
| Context | Single candle sweep | Consolidation/ranging breakout |
| Result | Returns to original direction | Reverses with expansion/speed |

Both trap traders on one side and move opposite.

*Teaching time: 6.0 min | Words: 900 | Frames: 1*

## FAIR VALUE GAP (FVG) — Refined Liquidity Void

*Teaching time: 5.0 min | Words: 700 | Frames: 1*

**FVG = Refined form of Liquidity Void. Measured WICK to WICK.**

- Gap between candle 1's wick and candle 3's wick (skip middle candle)
- Candle COLOR does NOT matter
- Only trade in trend direction
- Only trade AFTER liquidity sweep or grab
- Couple with OB or S&D for highest probability

## VALID FVG RULES (Critical for Success)

*Teaching time: 12.0 min | Words: 2,000 | Frames: 2*

**Not every FVG is valid. The extreme candle rule determines validity.**

### Sell/Bearish Trend:
- Draw FVG from **HIGHEST candle's wick** → VALID
- FVG from any other candle → **INVALID**

### Buy/Bullish Trend:
- Draw FVG from **LOWEST candle's wick** → VALID
- FVG from any other candle → **INVALID**

### Why Traders Fail:
- They draw FVG from random candles, not the extreme candle
- Market doesn't reach their FVG → it was invalid
- The real valid FVG (from extreme candle) was closer to price

### Additional Rules:
- Pullback must be VALID before finding FVG in it
- After first valid FVG: subsequent FVGs in same swing = also valid
- Candle color irrelevant — only extreme wick position matters
