## MARKET STRUCTURE MAPPING (Foundation)

*Teaching time: 5.0 min | Words: 600 | Frames: 3*

**Market Structure = Market Trend.** The foundation of all ICT/SMC trading.

- **Bullish Trend**: Market creates Higher Highs (HH) and Higher Lows (HL)
- **Bearish Trend**: Market creates Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL)
- Knowing the actual HH/HL/LH/LL accounts for **50% of trading success**
- Without trend knowledge, you cannot effectively buy or sell in any market

**Key Insight**: The same price structure is mapped COMPLETELY differently by retail traders vs smart money traders.

## INDUCEMENT RULE (The Key Differentiator)

*Teaching time: 4.0 min | Words: 600 | Frames: 4*

**Inducement** is the first pullback on the LEFT side of the current high.

### Two Rules for Valid Higher High:

1. **Rule 1**: Market *must take out* the first (most recent) inducement from the Previous Swing
   - "Take out" = price breaks below the inducement level
   - Inducement = first pullback to the left of current high

2. **Rule 2**: Candle *must close* BELOW Inducement AND *above* the High
   - Both conditions required simultaneously
   - If candle doesn't close above high → it's a **Liquidity Sweep**, not a valid HH

### What Happens Without Inducement:
- If no inducement was available (no previous swing pullback) → Smart Money does NOT consider it a valid HH/HL
- The swing is marked with X (invalid)

## LIQUIDITY SWEEP vs VALID HH

*Teaching time: 4.0 min | Words: 500 | Frames: 3*

### Liquidity Sweep:
- Candle sweeps below inducement but does NOT close above high
- Market just collects pending orders (sell stops, breakeven) and returns
- This is NOT a valid Higher High

### Liquidity Definition:
- Pending Orders, Stop Losses, Breakeven levels
- Smart Money targets pools of liquidity to execute large orders

### Real Chart Application:
The video demonstrates on a candlestick chart:
1. First high → **Liquidity Sweep** (no inducement taken)
2. Market takes inducement → closes above → **valid HH confirmed**
3. New inducement forms at next pullback
4. Process repeats → next **Higher High (HH)** and **Higher Low (HL)** confirmed

### Entry Strategy (Preview):
- After confirming HH/HL via inducement rules
- Look for buy entry on pullback BELOW the inducement zone
- This dramatically improves success rate vs retail approach
