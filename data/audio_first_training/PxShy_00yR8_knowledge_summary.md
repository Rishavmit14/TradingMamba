## FAIR VALUE GAP (FVG) — Review

*Teaching time: 4.0 min | Words: 800 | Frames: 2*

**FVG = Refined Form of Liquidity Void or Price Imbalance. Wick-to-Wick gap between 1st and 3rd candle.**

- Area where price moves sharply in one direction, leaving pending orders behind as liquidity
- Measured specifically as wick-to-wick gap (not body-to-body)
- Inside candle FVG = NOT important (no genuine imbalance)
- FVG is HIGHEST priority POI zone — purest form of price imbalance with unmitigated SM orders

## ORDER BLOCK — Review

*Teaching time: 4.0 min | Words: 800 | Frames: 2*

**OB = Refined form of IOF = Collection of pending orders of big banks distributed in block form.**

- Bearish OB = buy candle before sell move; Bullish OB = sell candle before buy move
- Valid OB Rule 1: candle MUST sweep liquidity of previous candle or previous high/low
- Valid OB Rule 2: price imbalance (FVG) MUST exist below/above the candle's wick
- Inside candle = NOT valid pullback → cannot form valid OB
- OB in inducement/engineered liquidity zone = Smart Money Trap
- Order Block is 2nd priority POI zone (after FVG)

## QML LEVEL (QUASIMODO LEVEL) — New Concept

*Teaching time: 8.0 min | Words: 1,800 | Frames: 8*

**QML Level = price level where market sweeps liquidity above/below a high/low and moves sharply opposite.**

- Named after Disney's Quasimodo — pattern resembles uneven shoulders (one higher than the other)
- **Bearish QML**: Price sweeps high's liquidity (liquidity grab) → MSS below corresponding low → horizontal line at swept high = QML entry
- **Bullish QML**: Price sweeps low's liquidity → MSS above corresponding high → horizontal line at swept low = QML entry
- MSS (Market Structure Shift) = candle body must CLOSE beyond the key level (not just wick)
- QML is 3rd priority POI zone — used when no valid FVG or OB available
- Provides excellent risk/reward ratio
- High probability when inducement is also available at QML level
- QML can align with IOF baseline for stronger confluence

### Real Chart Example 1: USD/CAD M30 (Bullish QML)
- Market swept low liquidity → shift confirmed → QML level identified at swept low
- QML aligned with IOF baseline → very strong confluence
- Entry at QML level with approximately 1:5 risk-reward ratio
- Market respected QML and moved up significantly

### Real Chart Example 2: AUD/USD M15 (Bearish QML)
- BOS (Break of Structure) confirmed bearish trend
- IDM/SMT zone identified where retail OBs were trapped
- Internal liquidity sweep provided additional confirmation
- QML Level drawn at the swept internal liquidity high

## ENTRY WORKFLOW WITH POI ZONES

*Teaching time: 6.0 min | Words: 1,200 | Frames: 7*

**NEVER trade the first POI zone directly. Wait for inducement → structure shift → NEW zone → entry.**

### Complete Workflow Steps:
1. Identify overall market structure (trend direction)
2. Identify the first POI zone (FVG/OB/QML) — do NOT trade it
3. Wait for inducement to form at/near the POI zone
4. Wait for Market Structure Shift (MSS) — candle body close beyond key level
5. After shift, identify NEW POI zone (priority: FVG → OB → QML)
6. Enter trade at the new zone with stop-loss beyond the shift point

### Smart Money Trap Warning:
- OBs below inducement in a sell trend = Smart Money Traps
- Retail traders enter at these OBs and get stopped out
- When OBs are trapped, use QML Level as the POI entry instead

### Key Confluences:
- QML aligned with IOF baseline = very high probability
- Internal liquidity sweeps within structure = additional confirmation
- Inducement available at QML level = higher probability

## Video Summary

This eighteenth video introduces the three Point of Interest (POI) zones used for entry planning in SMC trading: FVG (1st priority), Order Block (2nd priority), and QML Level (3rd priority). The presenter reviews FVG as a refined form of liquidity void (wick-to-wick gap between 1st and 3rd candle) and Order Block as a refined form of IOF (valid when sweep + FVG rules met). The new concept is QML Level (Quasimodo Level) — named after Disney's Quasimodo character due to the uneven shoulder pattern. A QML forms when price sweeps liquidity above/below a level and reverses with a Market Structure Shift. The horizontal line at the swept level becomes the QML entry point. QML is used when no valid FVG or OB is available and provides excellent risk/reward. The critical entry workflow: NEVER trade the first POI zone → wait for inducement → wait for structure shift → trade the NEW zone that forms after the shift. Two complete real-chart examples demonstrate the workflow: USD/CAD M30 bullish QML trade (1:5 R:R, QML aligned with IOF) and AUD/USD M15 bearish QML trade (IDM/SMT identification, internal liquidity sweep, QML entry with large profit).
