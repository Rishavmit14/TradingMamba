## HIGH PROBABILITY INDUCEMENT (Core Teaching)

*Teaching time: 5.0 min | Words: 600 | Frames: 2*

**High Probability Inducement = MAJOR inducement (deepest pullback). 80-85% probability.**

### Three Rules:

| Scenario | High Probability IDM |
|----------|---------------------|
| Single inducement | That one IS the high probability |
| Multiple, different sizes | MAJOR (deepest) is high probability |
| Multiple, SAME size | FIRST one is high probability |

### Size Correlation:
- **Minor inducement** → minor market reaction (small move)

### Entry Planning:
- Find major inducement → look for order block below it → **80-85% high probability setup**

## ENGINEERED LIQUIDITY — DEFINITION (New Concept)

*Teaching time: 4.0 min | Words: 500 | Frames: 1*

**Engineered Liquidity = Smart Money trap designed for retail support/resistance traders.**

### What It Looks Like:
- **Equal lows** (double/triple bottom) → retail sees "strong support" → engineered liquidity
- **Equal highs** (double/triple top) → retail sees "strong resistance" → engineered liquidity
- **Trendline channels/flags** → retail sees "pattern breakout" → engineered liquidity

### How It Works:
1. Market creates equal highs/lows or trendline patterns
2. Retail traders place orders at these "support/resistance" levels
3. Smart Money sweeps these levels → collects all pending orders
4. Market moves in the OPPOSITE direction of retail's expectation
