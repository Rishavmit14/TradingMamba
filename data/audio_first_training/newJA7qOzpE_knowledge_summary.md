## Overview
This lecture teaches the complete Liquidity Sweep Based Entry Module — also known as Turtle Soup, Sweep Based Entry, Manipulation, Liquidity Hunt, and Stop Hunt. The presenter traces the concept back to its origin: Sir Richard Wyckoff's research (1896/1912), explaining why liquidity sweeps happen, how Smart Money uses them, and how to trade them with a simplified entry technique called SBC (Sweep Based Change of Character).

## Concepts Covered (5)

### 1. Liquidity Sweep Theory & Wyckoff Origin
- Institutional traders need large liquidity pools to execute massive orders
- They sweep the side with MORE orders (buy stops above, sell stops below ranges)
- **If market wants to BUY → sweeps sell side (low manipulation)**
- **If market wants to SELL → sweeps buy side (high manipulation)**
- Sweep moves are always QUICK and EFFICIENT — SM footprints
- Original research: **Sir Richard Wyckoff (1896/1912)** — all other names are derivatives

### 2. Wyckoff Distribution Schematic Applied
- **Climax** → **AR (Automatic Rally)** → **Test** → **ST (Secondary Test)** → **UTAD (Upthrust)** → **Markdown**
- Ranges are INTENTIONAL — SM wants retail traders to build liquidity on both sides
- Upthrust = fast breakout above range → traps breakout buyers → SM trap
- Distribution is only VALID when AR bottom breaks after upthrust
- The schematic explains every liquidity sweep's "why"

### 3. Redistribution & Re-Accumulation Modules
- **Sell Module (Redistribution)**: Drop → Range → Sweep HIGH → Drop → repeat
- **Buy Module (Re-accumulation)**: Rally → Range → Sweep LOW → Rally → repeat
- Sell trend: wait for sweep of OLD HIGHS / range high / daily-weekly candle high
- Buy trend: wait for sweep of OLD LOWS / range low / daily-weekly candle low
- OB or FVG outside the range = high probability entry zone

### 4. SBC — Sweep Based Change of Character (Entry Module)
**The core simplified technique:**
- **Rule**: Sweep one side + First candle body close on opposite side = trade direction
- **Buy SBC**: Low swept → candle body closes above swept swing's high → BUY
- **Sell SBC**: High swept → candle body closes below swept swing's low → SELL
- **Two-sided sweep**: whichever side gets first candle body close = direction
- **Entry**: 50% Fibonacci of sweep swing OR first FVG within the swing
- **Without inducement**: valid on major liquidity sweeps (daily/weekly candle extremes)
- **SL philosophy**: Safe SL > Tight SL — always add 4-6 pips for broker spread
- **TP**: minimum 1:3 R:R, target QML or previous swing 50%

## Key Takeaways
1. **Liquidity sweeps are Wyckoff's theory simplified** — same logic, easier application
2. **SBC is the simplest form**: sweep side + closing side = trade direction
3. **No structure knowledge needed** — pure sweep-based trading works independently
4. **Structure + Sweep = best results** — SBC with BOS/CHoCH confirmation = highest probability
5. **Works on ANY timeframe** — same logic from M5 to Monthly
6. **Counter-trend possible** — use LTF (M5/M15) for SBC refinement when trading against trend
7. **Major sweeps don't need inducement** — direct entry valid on daily/weekly candle extreme sweeps

## Trading Framework Summary
```

2. Wait for range → liquidity sweep of range extreme / old high-low / daily candle extreme
3. Check candle body closing side → confirms SBC
4. Entry: 50% fib OR first FVG of sweep swing (or OB/Hidden OB within zone)
5. SL: Beyond sweep extreme + 4-6 pip spread
```
