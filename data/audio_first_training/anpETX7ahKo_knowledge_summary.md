## PD ZONE INTRODUCTION

*Teaching time: 4.0 min | Words: 600 | Frames: 2*

**Premium & Discount is the basic principle behind all forex buying/selling.**

- Used as **CONFLUENCE** (not confirmation) in trade setups
- **Bullish trend**: Buy in discount = HIGH probability; Sell in premium = LOW probability
- **Bearish trend**: Sell in discount = HIGH probability; Buy in premium = LOW probability
- Filters out low probability setups → improves risk:reward
- **Highly Effective** on HTF (D1, H4, H1) / Swing Trading
- **Less Effective** on LTF (M10, M5, M3) / Scalping

## FIBONACCI RETRACEMENT TOOL

*Teaching time: 3.0 min | Words: 400 | Frames: 1*

**Fibonacci measures retracement depth after expansion and identifies PD zones.**

| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 0% | Swing extreme (high in buy, low in sell) |
| 38% | Shallow retracement |
| **50%** | **EQUILIBRIUM — no trade here** |
| 62% | Deep discount zone |
| 79% | Very deep discount zone |
| 100% | Swing origin |

## BUY TREND PD ZONES

*Teaching time: 3.0 min | Words: 500 | Frames: 1*

**Apply Fib from LOW→HIGH. Below 50% = Discount (buy). Above 50% = Premium.**

1. Apply Fibonacci from swing low (100%) to swing high (0%)
2. Wait for market to retrace below 50% → Discount Zone
3. Find POI on left side (OB, Demand, FVG, Imbalance)
4. Design buy setup → sync with bullish trend continuation

**Rule**: OBs/FVGs in discount zone = HIGH probability. In premium zone = LOW probability.

## SELL TREND PD ZONES

- Above 50% = Discount Zone (sell here — higher price = better short entry)

## Video Summary

This twelfth video teaches Premium & Discount (PD) Zones using the Fibonacci Retracement Tool. PD zones are used as CONFLUENCE (not confirmation) in buy/sell setups. The Fibonacci tool measures retracement depth after expansion, with 50% as the equilibrium (no trade zone). In buy trends, apply Fib low→high: below 50% = discount zone (buy, high probability), above 50% = premium (low probability). In sell trends, apply Fib high→low: above 50% = discount zone (sell, high probability), below 50% = premium (low probability). The key insight is that any POI (order blocks, FVGs, demand/supply zones) found in the discount zone is high probability, while those in the premium zone are low probability. PD zones are highly effective on HTF (D1, H4, H1) but less effective on LTF scalping.
