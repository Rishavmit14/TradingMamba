## COUNTER-TREND RULES (BEARISH MARKET EXAMPLE)

*Teaching time: 7.0 min | Words: 1,500 | Frames: 2*

**3 Rules: (1) Major trend candle body close above IDM, (2) FVG in that swing, (3) LTF confirmation (MSS/SCOB/CHoCH).**

- Rule 1: Major trend candle body close ABOVE its first valid IDM → counter-trend starts
- Rule 2: FVG must exist in the swing that closed above IDM → high probability
- Rule 3: LTF entry confirmation needed (MSS, SCOB, or swing-based CHoCH)
- Range = rejected high ↔ IDM close low → draw Premium/Discount zones (50%)
- Counter-trend target = FIRST sell zone above IDM or 50% equilibrium
- NEVER hold beyond first opposing zone — take profit and exit
- LTF BOS confirmed → deeper pullback → continue counter-trend entries
- Bullish counter-trend (bearish pullback in bullish market) = exact mirror
