## LIQUIDITY SWEEP RULES (Most Relaxed)

*Teaching time: 3.0 min | Words: 400 | Frames: 1*

**Liquidity Sweep = ONLY wicks matter. No body close required anywhere.**

| Rule | Liquidity Sweep |
|------|----------------|
| Inducement | NOT compulsory |
| Below Inducement | Only WICK counts; body close = NOT valid |
| Above High | Only WICK counts; body close = NOT valid |

- If candle body closes above the high → NOT a liquidity sweep (becomes BOS or Swing)
- If candle body closes below inducement → NOT a liquidity sweep (becomes Swing)
- Key identifier: **ONLY wicks** — any body close disqualifies liquidity sweep

## BOS vs SWING HIGH/LOW RULES (Critical Difference)

*Teaching time: 4.0 min | Words: 600 | Frames: 1*

**BOS and Swing High/Low share 2 rules but differ on Rule 2.**

### Comparison Table:

| Rule | BOS | Swing High/Low |
|------|-----|----------------|
| 1. Inducement | MUST take out | MUST take out |
| 2. Below Inducement | Wick OR Body = BOTH valid | **ONLY Body = valid; Wick = INVALID** |
| 3. Above High | Body MUST close above | Body MUST close above |

### The Critical Insight:
- **Every Swing High/Low IS also a BOS** (body close below IDM satisfies both)
- **NOT every BOS is a Swing High/Low** (wick-only below IDM satisfies BOS but not Swing)

### When Body Closes Below Inducement:
→ Creates **BOTH** Swing High/Low AND BOS simultaneously

### When Only Wick Goes Below Inducement:
→ Creates **ONLY** BOS (not Swing High/Low)

## Video Summary

This sixth video resolves student confusion about differentiating Liquidity Sweep, Break of Structure (BOS), and Swing High/Low by presenting a parallel comparison of their rules. Three rules govern all three: (1) Inducement, (2) Candle Body Close Below Inducement, (3) Candle Body Close Above High. Liquidity Sweep is the most relaxed — inducement is NOT required and ONLY wicks matter (any body close disqualifies it). BOS requires inducement AND body close above high, but accepts EITHER wick or body below inducement. Swing High/Low is the strictest — requires inducement, body close BELOW inducement (wick INVALID), AND body close above high. The critical insight: every Swing High/Low is also a BOS, but not every BOS is a Swing High/Low. The difference lies in whether the candle body closed below inducement (Swing High/Low) or just wicked below (BOS only). Demonstrated on an AUD/USD 4H chart where all three concepts appear within the same bullish trend.
