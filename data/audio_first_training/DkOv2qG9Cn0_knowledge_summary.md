## INDUCEMENT / PULLBACK (Definition)

*Teaching time: 6.0 min | Words: 800 | Frames: 2*

**Inducement is a trap before an area of Order Block / Supply / Demand / P.O.I.**

### Swing Types:
- **Swing Without Inducement**: Single impulse candle, no correction (like news candles)
- **Swing With Inducement**: Price corrects/pulls back at least once before continuing

### Counting Inducement Points:
- 2 corrections within a swing = 2 inducement points
- 3 corrections = 3 inducement points
- **Retail mistake**: Counts internal pullbacks as separate swings (sees 2, 3, or 4 swings)
- **Smart Money truth**: ALL internal pullbacks are ONE single swing until inducement is TAKEN

### When Does a New Swing Start?
- A new swing ONLY starts when the market TAKES (breaks through) the inducement level
- Taking inducement = retail trader sees "breakout", sells/buys prematurely, gets trapped

## INDUCEMENT MECHANISM (How Smart Money Traps Retail)

*Teaching time: 5.0 min | Words: 600 | Frames: 1*

**The step-by-step mechanism of how inducement works:**

1. Price approaches an Order Block (Smart Money's buy zone)
2. Market creates an inducement trap ABOVE the order block
3. Retail traders do **"Early Buying"** — they buy at the inducement level
4. Retail places **Breakeven** at inducement and **Stop Loss** below
5. Smart Money drives price DOWN through inducement
6. This **hits Breakeven** → then **hits Stop Loss** → clears all liquidity
7. Price reaches the actual **Order Block** where Smart Money's real orders are
8. The **actual bullish move** begins from the Order Block

### Why 70% of Traders Are Confused:
- They think every bearish candle before a bullish move is an Order Block
- Without understanding structure mapping + inducement, you can't determine valid Order Blocks

*Teaching time: 6.0 min | Words: 700 | Frames: 1*

**This ties inducement back to structure mapping from Video 1.**

### Two Conditions for Valid Higher High (Bullish):
1. **Condition 1**: Price must TAKE the inducement (break below the first pullback on left side of high)
2. **Condition 2**: Candle must close ABOVE the previous high AND BELOW the inducement level

### If Conditions Fail:
- Candle creates only a wick above high but no close → **Liquidity Sweep**, NOT valid HH
- No inducement within current swing → Inducement **transfers** from previous swing

### Inducement Location Rule:
- **ALWAYS** the first pullback on the **LEFT side** of the current HIGH
- In bullish trend: look at HIGH's left side (NOT the low side)
- If current swing has no internal pullback, look at the PREVIOUS swing's inducement

### Real Chart Application:
1. Market breaks a high → retail thinks "support formed" → buys → places breakeven

3. Price reaches Order Block → Smart Money enters → actual move starts
4. **Higher High confirmed** (inducement taken + candle close conditions met)

## Video Summary

This second video introduces the two fundamental concepts that drive Smart Money trading: Liquidity and Inducement. Liquidity is defined as all pending orders, stop losses, and breakeven levels in the market — pools of liquidity form at equal highs (retail resistance) and equal lows (retail support). Smart money (banks with billions/trillions) needs this liquidity as counterparties to execute large orders. Inducement is the trap Smart Money sets before an Order Block — any pullback or correction within a swing creates an inducement point. Retail traders mistake these pullbacks for breakouts and enter prematurely ("Early Buying"), placing breakevens and stop losses that Smart Money then sweeps to collect liquidity before entering at the actual Order Block. The video integrates these concepts back into the structure mapping from V01, showing that HH/HL are only confirmed when: (1) inducement is taken, and (2) candle closes above high and below inducement. Without inducement, swings are invalid. The concept of inducement transfer (when no internal pullback exists) is previewed for V03.
