## PULLBACK DEFINITION (Introduction)

*Teaching time: 2.0 min | Words: 300 | Frames: 1*

**A Pullback occurs when the Price Takes a Break and corrects against the trend.**

- Also known as **Correction**
- **Temporary dip** in a generally upward-trending market (bullish)
- **Temporary rally** in a generally downward-trending market (bearish)
- Pullbacks are short-term — market resumes original direction after correction
- Knowledge of VALID pullback is the key differentiator taught in this video

## LIQUIDITY SWEEP (Prerequisite Knowledge)

*Teaching time: 6.0 min | Words: 900 | Frames: 3*

**Liquidity sweeps are used by institutional traders/banks to execute large orders.**

### Key Properties:
- Sweeps are **Quick and Efficient** — too fast for retail to catch without knowledge
- Smart Money leaves **footprints** at sweep locations
- Retail trader volume = barely 1-2% of institutional volume

### How Sweeps Work:
1. Market builds a range with HIGH and LOW
2. Breakout traders pile buy stops above HIGH and sell stops below LOW
3. Banks target the side with MORE orders (larger liquidity pool)
4. **Sell Side Sweep**: Market breaks below LOW, collects sell stops, reverses UP
5. **Buy Side Sweep**: Market breaks above HIGH, collects buy stops, reverses DOWN

### Candlestick Form:
- **Sell side sweep** = Long lower wick on bullish candle
- **Buy side sweep** = Long upper wick on bearish candle
- Cross (**X**) mark placed on swept side = "market has done its work here"

### Why This Matters:
Liquidity sweep knowledge is essential for **EVERYTHING** going forward:
- Inducement validity
- Order Block validation
- Fibonacci corrections
- Market setups and entries

## VALID PULLBACK RULES (Core Teaching)

*Teaching time: 7.0 min | Words: 1,200 | Frames: 3*

**This is the SINGLE most important concept in this lecture.**

### Definition:
When Price Takes out (Sweeps) the **Previous Candle's Low or High**, it is called a **Valid Pullback**.

### Rules by Market Direction:

**Bullish Market Pullback:**
- **LOW of the Highest Candle** must be broken (swept) to validate pullback

**Bearish Market Pullback:**
- **HIGH of the Lowest Candle** must be broken (swept) to validate pullback

### Three Validation Rules:
1. Next candle must sweep/break the previous candle's low (bullish) or high (bearish)
2. **Both body close (body base sweep) AND wick (wick base sweep) validate** the pullback
3. It is **NOT necessary** for next candle to close/break the high/low in the OPPOSITE direction

### How to Apply:
1. **Step 1**: Find the HIGHEST (bullish) or LOWEST (bearish) candle in the pullback
2. **Step 2**: Check if next candle(s) broke its low (bullish) or high (bearish)
3. If broken → **VALID** pullback. If not → **INVALID** pullback

### Bearish Market Examples (Candlestick):
| Example | Candles | Result | Why |
|---------|---------|--------|-----|
| Single base | Lowest #1, #2 breaks high | VALID ✓ | High of lowest candle broken |
| Two-candle | Lowest #3, #4 breaks high | VALID ✓ | High of lowest candle broken |
| Three+ candle | #5,6,7,8 | INVALID ✗ | Lowest candle's high NOT broken |
| Tricky (A,B,C,D) | D is actual lowest (not C) | VALID ✓ | Must find REAL lowest candle |

### Critical Insights:
- **Inside bars** (candles within a larger candle) have NO significance
- **Candle color** (bullish/bearish) does NOT matter — only position (highest/lowest) matters
- The tricky A,B,C,D case: must identify the ACTUAL lowest candle, not just assume

## VALID INDUCEMENT (Culminating Concept)

*Teaching time: 5.0 min | Words: 800 | Frames: 4*

**Valid Inducement = First Valid Pullback on the left side of the recent High or Low.**

### Definition:
Inducement is the **first Valid Pullback** on the left side of the recent High or recent Low. It is **a trap before an area of POI** (Point of Interest = Order Block / Supply / Demand zone).

### The Key Rule:
**For inducement to be valid, the pullback MUST be valid first.**

### Process to Find Valid Inducement:
1. Find the **highest point** (bullish) or **lowest point** (bearish)
2. Look on its **LEFT side**
3. Find the **first pullback**
4. Check if that pullback is **valid** using candle rules
5. If valid → that's the **inducement**
6. If NOT valid → skip it → find next valid pullback → **inducement SHIFTS** there

### Example Walkthrough:
- Chart shows two "Highest Candle" points
- **Upper pullback**: Highest candle's low NOT broken by next candle → pullback INVALID → inducement INVALID (✗)
- **Lower pullback**: This highest candle's low WAS broken → pullback VALID → inducement VALID (✓)
- Inducement shifted from invalid upper point to valid lower point

### What This Means for Trading:
- Order Block at INVALID inducement = **Smart Money Trap** (NOT tradeable)
- FVG at inducement zone = part of inducement → NOT tradeable
- Understanding this: success rate **50% → 80%**, entries **100 → 70** (quality filter)
- Find entry BELOW the valid inducement (for buy), ABOVE for sell
