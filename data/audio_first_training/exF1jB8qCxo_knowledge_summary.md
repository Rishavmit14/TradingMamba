## STRUCTURE MAPPING TECHNIQUES

*Teaching time: 12.0 min | Words: 2,500 | Frames: 4*

**Two techniques for starting structure mapping on any chart.**

### Technique 1 (Expert):
- Open chart → zoom out → find the LAST Change of Character point
- Start mapping from that CHoCH point forward
- Key insight: even if visible chart looks bullish, major structure may be bearish

### Technique 2 (Beginner-Friendly):
- Cannot immediately find CHoCH? → Zoom out to complete historical data
- Go far back, find clear bearish or bullish trend
- Start mapping from there using BOS rules:
  - Candle body closes beyond inducement → BOS + swing point confirmed
  - Mark LLs, LHs (bearish) or HHs, HLs (bullish)
  - When market breaks Lower High → CHoCH → start bullish mapping
  - When market breaks Higher Low → CHoCH → start bearish mapping

### Critical Rules:
- NEVER build perception from visible chart — always zoom out first
- BOS: candle body must close beyond inducement level
- Inducement is ABOVE in bearish trend, BELOW in bullish trend
- Same rules apply to ANY timeframe: M5, M15, H1, H4, 2R, Daily

## RANGE DEFINITION & PREMIUM/DISCOUNT ZONES

*Teaching time: 6.0 min | Words: 1,000 | Frames: 2*

**Range is defined when market closes beyond 1st valid inducement.**

- **Bearish**: Close Above IDM → range from High to Low → Premium above 50%, Discount below
- **Bullish**: Close Below IDM → range from Low to High → Premium above 50%, Discount below
- **Sell entries** only in Premium zone (above 50%)
- **Buy entries** only in Discount zone (below 50%)
- **Level Qualification**: in sell market — swing where previous high liquidity was swept + drop = qualified level
- NEVER enter directly at the zone → enter at qualified level WITHIN PD zone

## ENTRY CONFIRMATION TECHNIQUES (3 Techniques)

*Teaching time: 8.0 min | Words: 1,800 | Frames: 3*

**Three techniques for confirming entry when market reaches HTF sell/buy zone.**

### 1. Market Structure Shift (MSS) — 80-88% accuracy
- Market sweeps previous high's liquidity → candle body CLOSES above
- Expansion leg drops below the low → MSS confirmed
- **WAIT for inducement** to form after MSS
- Enter at FVG in the expansion leg
- MSS = strongest technique

### 2. Sweep Based Change of Character (SBC)
- Market sweeps previous high's liquidity → candle WICK only (body does NOT close above)
- Opposite side close → SBC confirmed
- **NO inducement wait needed** — already swept major liquidity
- Enter directly at first FVG after SBC

### 3. Swing Based Change of Character
- Map LTF (M5/M15) structure inside HTF zone
- When HTF zone taps → LTF Higher Low breaks → CHoCH
- Enter at first FVG/OB/Supply zone after CHoCH

### Key Differences:
| Feature | MSS | SBC | Swing CHoCH |
|---------|-----|-----|-------------|
| Liquidity sweep | Body CLOSE above | Wick ONLY | N/A (uses LTF structure) |
| Inducement wait | YES | NO | NO (already in HTF zone) |
| Entry zone | FVG in expansion leg | First FVG after SBC | First FVG/OB after CHoCH |
| Accuracy | 80-88% | High | Moderate |

**Critical Rule**: NEVER enter directly in HTF zone → enter at NEW zone formed after the reaction.
