**The setup is driven by Order Block Theory: liquidity grab/sweep → expansion → close below middle low.**

- Two models: **Liquidity Grab** (body close above previous high) and **Liquidity Sweep** (wick only)

- The zone that grabbed/swept liquidity = tradeable sell/buy zone
- **Expansion = Smart Money footprints** — where SM entered the market
- Apply anywhere market grabs or sweeps liquidity

## MARKET STRUCTURE SHIFT (MSS) — NOT the Same as BOS

*Teaching time: 4.0 min | Words: 600 | Frames: 2*

**MSS is DIFFERENT from Break of Structure. MSS has only 3 rules.**

### MSS 3 Rules:
1. Market must **take out liquidity** (grab or sweep)
2. Market must move in **expansion form** (sharp movement with speed/displacement)
3. Market must **close below/above the middle low/high**

When all 3 met → MSS confirmed → the liquidity-grabbing zone = valid entry zone.
