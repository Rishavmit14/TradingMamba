## RULE 1: WEAK SWING POINT

*Teaching time: 6.0 min | Words: 900 | Frames: 2*

**A weak swing point looks valid but its strength was consumed by a Liquidity Sweep.**

When the Swing Initiating Candle (SIC) that forms a swing high/low is also the candle sweeping prior liquidity, that swing point is "weak." Its directional energy was used for the sweep, not for building genuine structure. Breaking a weak swing point produces a FAKE CHoCH.

### Key Identification:
- Check HOW the swing point was formed
- Was the swing-initiating candle simultaneously sweeping liquidity at the other end?
- If yes → weak swing point → any CHoCH from breaking it is FAKE
- The sweeping candle = candle that is part of liquidity sweep AND creates the swing

## RULE 2: SWING POINT CREATING ENGINEERED LIQUIDITY

*Teaching time: 6.0 min | Words: 900 | Frames: 1*

**If a swing point creates equal highs/lows (engineered liquidity), breaking it = FAKE CHoCH.**

When a swing point sits at a level with equal highs or equal lows, it forms an engineered liquidity zone. Breaking this level is NOT a genuine trend change — it's a liquidity sweep. Retail traders see "strong support broken = trend reversal" but Smart Money is just collecting pending orders.

### The Trap:
1. Swing point creates equal lows → retail sees "strong support"
2. Price breaks below → retail sees CHoCH (bearish)
3. Smart Money sweeps the accumulated sell stops/buy orders
4. Price reverses back up → retail was trapped

**Visual**: AUD/USD 4H chart showing swing points with equal lows that get swept — fake CHoCH.

## RULE 3: SWING HIGH/LOW ACTING AS INDUCEMENT

*Teaching time: 6.0 min | Words: 900 | Frames: 1*

**When price moves in IMPULSE (no pullback), the origin acts as inducement — breaking it = FAKE CHoCH.**

If price moves from a low to a high with NO pullback (straight impulse move), that low becomes an inducement level. The market MUST return to take this inducement before continuing. When price returns and breaks that level, retail sees CHoCH, but it's actually the market collecting required inducement.

### How to Check:
- Was there ANY pullback between the swing low and swing high?
- **No pullback** (impulse) → the low is inducement → breaking it = fake CHoCH
- **Pullback exists** → the swing point is valid → breaking it could be real CHoCH

**Visual**: AUD/USD 1D chart showing impulse move from low to high with no pullback, low labeled as inducement, "CHoCH?" annotation marked as fake.

## RULE 4: LOW TIMEFRAME SWING = HIGHER TIMEFRAME INDUCEMENT

*Teaching time: 6.0 min | Words: 900 | Frames: 1*

**A lower TF swing point may be a higher TF inducement level — making lower TF CHoCH fake.**

This is the most advanced rule. A swing point on M15/H1 may actually be an inducement level on H4/D1. When the lower timeframe breaks this swing point and shows CHoCH, it looks valid on that timeframe. But on the higher timeframe, this same level is just inducement being taken — the higher TF trend continues.

### Multi-Timeframe Hierarchy:
- D1 > H4 > H1 > M15 — higher timeframe ALWAYS dominates
- M15/H1 CHoCH can be fake if the swing point is H4/D1 inducement
- Single-timeframe traders get trapped — they can't see the higher TF context
- **Solution**: Always check if your swing point is a higher TF inducement level
